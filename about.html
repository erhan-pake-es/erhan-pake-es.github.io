Who are we? 我們是誰？

想說中英卻怕尷尬？來語言交換社！我們每週在山海樓學餐聚會，提供自在友善的環境，帶你輕鬆結交各國學伴與跨國友誼。

Want to speak Mandarin or English but afraid of feeling awkward? Come to the Language Exchange Club! We meet weekly at the Shanhai Cafeteria, providing a comfortable and friendly environment to help you easily find international language buddies and build cross-cultural friendships.
